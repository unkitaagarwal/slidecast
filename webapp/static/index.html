How it works

From prompt to posted in three steps.

01

Pick a format

Deep Dive (10 slides) for one topic, fully unpacked. Listicle (12 slides, 5 items) for save-magnet content that travels across niches.

02

Type a prompt

"5 habits that quietly wreck your sleep" or "cozy fall dinners for cold rainy nights." Gemini writes 5 detailed entries with hooks, key points, and step-by-step content for any domain.

03

Get the carousel

Nano Banana renders cinematic visuals. Compositor lays out detail pages and your CTA, then uploads the whole carousel to Firebase — ready to schedule.
