The framework

        
From brief to posted. One pipeline.

        
Most tools solve content or distribution. Slidecast does both — one workflow, every account, zero copy-paste.

      

      

        

          
01

          
Type a brief

          
Pick a format, type one line — "5 weekend dinners for your lazy ass." That's the entire input. Slidecast handles everything downstream.

        

        

          
02

          
Auto-generate

          
Gemini writes the recipes, caption, and hashtags. Nano Banana renders cinematic food photos. The compositor lays out 10–12 finished slides in ~30 seconds.

        

        

          
03

          
Auto-post everywhere

          
Postiz pushes the carousel to every connected TikTok and Instagram account on schedule — captions and hashtags already in place. No re-uploading, no re-captioning.
